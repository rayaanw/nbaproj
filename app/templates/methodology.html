Calibration

Predicted probability (xFG%) vs. actual make rate, bucketed. A well-calibrated model's curve tracks the diagonal closely — this matters here because xFG% is shown directly to users.

Data Limitations

nba_api's public endpoints do not expose true per-shot defender distance, shot clock, or a contested-shot flag — that tracking data isn't publicly available. Action type (pullup, catch-and-shoot, layup, dunk, step-back, etc.) is used as the primary real per-shot proxy for how contested a shot likely was.

Defender distance is approximated with a league-average FG% prior, joined by (season, shot-distance bucket) from aggregated tracking dashboards — this is a supplementary approximation, not true per-shot tracking data, and should not be read as such.

Shot clock and a true contested-shot flag are not available at all from public endpoints and are not used anywhere in this model.

The model is evaluated chronologically — trained on the two oldest seasons in scope, tested on the most recent season entirely held out — to avoid leakage from within-season streaks and to mirror real deployment (predicting shots the model has never seen).
