NBA Shot Quality Model & Dashboard

A model that predicts the probability any given NBA shot goes in (expected FG%, xFG%) based on shot difficulty — independent of who took it — so you can compare a player's actual FG% against their expected FG% to isolate pure shot-making skill from shot selection.
